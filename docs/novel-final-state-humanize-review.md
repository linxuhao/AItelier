# Novel final-state / humanize — implementer review

Scope: eleven loaded novel role templates, their runtime-wiring/static tests,
authored semantic evaluation cases, and documentation. No graph topology,
model/tool configuration, mechanical thresholds, checkpoint policy, database,
fiction content or live run was changed. This is implementer review, not the
platform director's independent acceptance.

## Findings resolved
- Negative editorial feedback becomes a constraint on the final story rather
  than a new narrated event, dialogue or explanatory denial.
- Reviewers check effective scoped meaning instead of requiring all historic
  conflicting instructions or matching quoted words mechanically.
- Revisions include affected causal dependencies; a removed threat cannot leave
  its unsupported injuries, reactions or consequences frozen by a local-edit rule.
- Pure editing leakage is not protected as unique fictional information, while
  meaningful negation, uncertainty, character beliefs and real one-off facts are.
- Humanize no longer unconditionally treats replacing disappointment with a new
  action/prop as harmless. It preserves emotional strength and knowledge scope.
- Removed unsupported claims about research-guaranteed convergence and the
  absence of a semantic comparison reviewer; retained the real fidelity gate.
- Ledger rules distinguish story facts, explicitly approved scoped canonical
  rulings and durable writing preferences. Future plans are not completed facts.

## Limits and integration observations
- The 14 semantic cases are authored evaluation rubrics, not observed model
  responses. Mocked provider tests prove instructions reach actual constructed
  JSON/native agents; they do not prove compliance, prose quality or live output.
- Inspected baseline novel_chapter gives draft_review explicit feedback_of=draft,
  but humanize/humanize_review do not receive all original checkpoint feedback.
  finalize_review receives probe/outline/humanize/finalize, not direct
  feedback_of=outline/draft. These are existing input limits; this prompt-only
  change does not invent missing inputs or trust a finalizer's self-attested
  rule source. Explicit canonical-rule changes may require the director to
  supply/check the original ruling or address context delivery during integration.
- No post-deploy behavioral sample or independent verifier was run here. Keep
  checkpoints and evaluate false positives on negative evidence, not just echoes.
- Existing mechanical length/paragraph gates can still expose irreconcilable
  draft defects. They must not be weakened or satisfied by fabricating prose.
- The baseline contains existing tool-edit instructions in novel_design. A
  separate platform tool-contract migration owns those API instructions. Preserve
  that migration when combining this semantic patch with a newer main.
- Integration with the optional director_brief candidate must preserve both its
  run-scoped input contract and these final-state/humanize instructions. Its
  acceptance/deployment is separately owned; do not supersede its artifact.
